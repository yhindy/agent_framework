## Gaps and Recommendations

### Minor Gaps (Non-Blocking)

1. **Rules Generation Detail**: The engineering design mentions `generateRulesMarkdown()` but does not provide the exact markdown template or transformation logic. This is acceptable as it will be defined during implementation, and the test cases outline expected behavior.

2. **Template Sharing**: The design supports templates but does not specify whether templates can be shared between users/projects or are only local. Consider adding this in a future iteration.

3. **Migration Path**: Section 9 mentions migration for existing super minions but implementation details are deferred. The version field in configs enables future migrations, which is sufficient.

### Alignment with UX Design

The UX design fully complements the engineering design:
- Entry point and component hierarchy match
- TypeScript interfaces in UX Section 12 align with Engineering Section 2
- Implementation phases (UX Section 17) align with Engineering Section 6
